# MyAmbagan is a ledger only and never handles money

MyAmbagan records who paid, who owes whom, and who confirmed receiving repayment. Actual payment happens outside the app (GCash, cash, bank transfer), and the app does not hold funds, move money, or integrate with any payment provider. This keeps the product minimal and avoids financial licensing, compliance and custody risk. A "Paid" mark is a claim confirmed by the recipient, not a verified transaction.
